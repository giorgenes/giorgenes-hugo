## Audience

**Primary:** Early-stage startup founders who need a technical and design partner to go from idea to shipped product.  
**Secondary:** Potential employers evaluating engineering and design depth.

## Brand Voice

One word: **systemic** — structured, interconnected, methodical.

| Axis | Position |
|---|---|
| Serious ↔ Playful | Serious at a distance, playful up close |
| Corporate ↔ Human | Human / personal |
| Machine ↔ Organic | Machine — precise, geometric, engineered |
| Energy | Calm and methodical |

**What we want visitors to feel:** *"This person gets startups, moves fast, and won't let me make expensive mistakes."*  
**What we actively avoid:** Generic / forgettable.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Modern SaaS / startup.** Polished and confident without feeling corporate. Resonates with technical founders who appreciate craft.

Reference: [Ali Abdaal](https://aliabdaal.com) — clear above-the-fold message, strong call to action.  
Vibe: Pieter Levels — ships fast, no fluff, raw competence on display.

## Site Structure

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hero | Above-the-fold headline + availability badge + dual CTAs |
| Services | Product Engineering · Product Design · Technical Advisory |
| Experiments | Tagged posts (`experiments`) with demo links and screenshots |
| Writing | Blog posts — short punchy how-tos and founder insights |
| About | Bio, photo, LinkedIn + GitHub, primary CTA |
| Blog post | Single post with ToC, prose, code blocks, backlinks |

## Content Model

Notes are authored in **Obsidian** and synced to Hugo at build time via `scripts/sync-obsidian.py`.  
Only notes with `public: true` in front matter **or** the tag `public` are published.  
Content types are expressed through tags (e.g. `experiments`, `founders`, `ai`, `design`).
